My Library — Privacy policy

Effective: 16 October 2026

My Library is a public library of books and documents operated by the owner of the chinenglish1-source GitHub account.

Information and services

No reader account is required. The library has no advertising or developer-added analytics SDKs. It does not request your contacts, camera, microphone, or location.

Opening the library sends normal web requests to GitHub Pages. Fonts may load from Google Fonts, and PDF font resources may load from jsDelivr. These providers and your browser may process information such as your IP address, browser information, and requested URLs under their own policies. The developer does not receive a reader profile from the app.

GitHub privacy · Google Fonts privacy · jsDelivr privacy

On your device

The browser may store the catalogue, app resources, cover images, and browsing state locally. PDFs you download remain on your device. You control sharing through your device’s share sheet. We do not sell personal data.

Your choices

You can clear this site’s browser data, remove the installed web app, and delete downloaded documents. Android app users may also need to clear website data in their browser because the app opens a browser-based reader.

Public content

The documents are public and may be downloaded or shared. Do not submit private information through public support issues.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or content concerns, contact the operator through the library support page. Policy changes will be published here with an updated effective date.
